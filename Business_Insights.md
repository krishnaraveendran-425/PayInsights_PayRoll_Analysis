# PayInsight – Payroll Analysis: Business Insights

## Project Overview
PayInsight is a payroll data analysis project that uses Excel, MySQL, and Tableau Public to examine employee salaries, payroll expenditure, deductions, and overtime.

The project uses fictional employee data for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes.

## Key Analysis Areas

- **Payroll Overview:** Analysed total gross payroll, total deductions, total net payroll, and average net salary.
- **Department Analysis:** Compared payroll expenditure and average net salary across departments.
- **Employee Salary Analysis:** Identified employees with the highest gross and net salaries.
- **Overtime Analysis:** Examined overtime hours and overtime payments across departments.
- **Deduction Analysis:** Compared provident fund (PF), tax, and other deductions.
- **Data Validation:** Checked whether gross salary and net salary calculations were consistent with their component values.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Monitor payroll expenditure:** Review department-level payroll costs to support budgeting and resource planning.
2. **Review overtime patterns:** Examine departments with higher overtime hours and payments to understand workload distribution.
3. **Track salary differences:** Compare average net salaries across departments and job roles to understand compensation patterns.
4. **Monitor deductions:** Review payroll deductions to improve transparency and identify inconsistencies.
5. **Maintain data accuracy:** Validate salary calculations and ensure payroll records use consistent numeric formats.
